# UmeTrack FK core

Source: [facebookresearch/emg2pose](https://github.com/facebookresearch/emg2pose/tree/5f6f62b1a0a08426adffe55900842e75a8adb38c/emg2pose/UmeTrack), revision `5f6f62b1a0a08426adffe55900842e75a8adb38c`.

- `core/kinematics/umetrack/hand.py`, `hand_skinning.py`, and `pytorch3d_transforms_so3.py` are byte-for-byte copies of `lib/common/`. The latter is Meta's reduced PyTorch3D v0.7.1 SO(3) implementation.
- `generic_hand_model.json` retains every non-mesh field from `dataset/generic_hand_model.json`. Only `mesh_vertices`, `mesh_triangles`, and `dense_bone_weights` were removed; landmark FK uses none of them. The remaining numeric values are unchanged. Source rest positions are in millimetres.
- `generic_hand_mesh.json` supplies those three original mesh fields for motion visualization: 788 vertices, 1544 triangles and 17-bone weights. Its provenance records the pinned revision, full upstream source hash, and a canonical hash of the matching non-mesh FK profile. Numeric mesh values are copied without modification; the visualization adapter converts millimetres to metres. This generic surface is not participant-specific geometry.
- `core/kinematics/human.py` is our adapter. It converts geometry to metres, broadcasts the static model, and requires an explicit hand side. Right hands reflect the local wrist transform's x column once, matching UmeTrack's `lib/tracker/perspective_crop.py`. It avoids importing upstream `kinematics.py`, whose `NamedTuple | Any` runtime annotation is incompatible with our Python environment.
- Its `fingertip_frames()` reuses the upstream rigid `_hand_skinning_transform` to transport distal-link frames. The rest frame construction (+Y along positive flexion, +X along the distal direction projected perpendicular to Y, +Z by cross product) is our retargeting convention, not an upstream landmark orientation API. Right-hand frames reflect polar directions and angular axes with their respective signs so that their determinant remains +1. Original landmark FK output is unchanged.
- `core/kinematics/mesh.py` calls the original `_skin_points` with dense mesh weights and the same right-wrist reflection as landmark FK. Right-hand triangle winding is reversed once. Original rounded weights are preserved, including their tiny deviations from a row sum of one; no weight renormalization or guessed finger geometry is introduced.
